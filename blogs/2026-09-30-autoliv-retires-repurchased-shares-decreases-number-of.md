Autoliv Retires Repurchased Shares, Decreases Number of Issued Shares Autoliv retired 1,232,671 shares of common stock that had been repurchased during the quarter which resulted in a decrease in the issued shares. The Company now has a total of 74,421,702 issued shares of common stock of which 72,006,695 shares are outstanding. Each share of outstanding common stock is entitled to one vote.
